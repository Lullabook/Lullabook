# Story Format & Scope

Status: shipped/binding — reversible product decision, not an ADR, but still pins
cost/layout/prompt shape today.

Still-binding format rules:
- **Tonal scope:** broad, any-occasion personalized stories (milestones, lessons,
  holidays, bedtime) — bedtime is one prominent theme, not the whole product (R1
  narrows the *shipping* story type to Bedtime + restored Learning, but the underlying
  format below is unchanged).
- **Length:** fixed ~12 Pages per Storybook.
- **Text density:** 1-3 short, simple sentences per Page.
- **Audience:** read-aloud, ages ~1-6.
- **Language:** English only; localization deferred.
- Covers the Text + Illustration medium; Audio/Video are later roadmap layers (cut in R1).
